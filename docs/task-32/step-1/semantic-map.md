## The 90% hypothesis

The complete inputs contain broad common themes, but “90%” has no defined
denominator: characters, topics, individual clauses, or interchangeable operator
behavior give different answers. The reproducible character-sequence diagnostic
is **16.31%**; it is deliberately not a semantic-equivalence measure. It cannot
confirm or refute semantic overlap.

A narrower usable hypothesis is: *can one small loading algorithm serve both
projects while all current material obligations remain reachable under their
own authority?* The table supports that hypothesis as a design recommendation,
not proven model behavior. It rejects mechanical removal of 90% of either prompt:
typed/v3/v4 selection, first-verdict publication permission, shared topic adoption,
Step allocation, controller limits, merge/closure and corporate/reader boundaries
are material divergences. Similar headings do not make these clauses duplicates.

No consumer rule is removed here. For a future migration, classify each moved
clause as exact preservation, meaning-preserving reformulation, accepted semantic
change, omission/weakening or justified local binding. Source-to-output coverage
and actual loading/application must be independently assessed; residual old rules
cannot conceal a missing shared obligation.
