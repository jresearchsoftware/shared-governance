# Owner-facing finding preview scenarios

These are source-guidance walks under the admitted Step 5, not an independent
verdict or live ChatGPT behavior test. The same information stays in the published
negative assessment. Examples add no approval or waiting stage and grant no
publication permission. This producer reaches the rule through adopted Task
Handoff, its manual client binding and generated Instructions; editing optional
Independent Review does not adopt it.

## Confirmed finding with supported origin

An owner-facing preview of R1 can say:

> **R1 / P1. Origin:** introduced in Step 2 (`48a52029b2896d9f7e5a11ae75cce7622b937b2f`),
> incompletely corrected in Step 4. **Evidence:** reviewed Step 4 result
> `c15bd73c2525ede12c2037109f0cee3d6ef1f511`, compared with the Step 2 planner;
> the production loop requires readiness on every historical package transition.
> The focused real-Git-history reproducer rejects a ready, version-bumped repair
> at production planning because its accepted predecessor has no LICENSE.
> **Impact:** an otherwise eligible corrected release can remain pending; no live
> publication incident is established. **Remediation/acceptance:** keep full
> readiness on the current release and pre-merge check, compare historical payloads
> under historical policy, retain appropriate owner/CI eligibility, and demonstrate
> candidate success followed by accepted-source production success for the repair.

This reports a confirmed local failure with synthetic PR/CI evidence. It does not
claim live private-producer acceptance, legal rights or a remote publication test.

## Explicit undetermined origin

For a hypothetical distinct finding, a sufficient preview can say:

> **P2. Origin: undetermined** — the earlier executable result needed for comparison
> is unavailable; introduction has not been established. **Evidence:** at reviewed
> result `<exact result>`, the observed operator summary omits a required warning;
> the earlier summary has not been inspected. **Impact:** an operator could infer
> success without the qualification; no actual incorrect release is established.
> **Remediation/acceptance:** render the required safe warning and supply a focused
> operator-output check at the corrected result. Resolve origin only if the missing
> earlier evidence becomes available; do not call this a regression meanwhile.

The placeholder identifies an illustrative scenario, not a discovered repository
defect. Explicit undetermined plus the gap is sufficient provenance; it does not
require unlimited historical investigation.

## Incomplete preview and publication consistency

“P1: old versions block release; fix the loop” lacks explicit origin, exact
comparison, qualified evidence/impact and closure evidence. It is an incomplete
report even if a later publication might supply them. Add the missing content to
the existing owner report; do not invent another checklist, reviewer, approval or
mandatory pause. A complete preview does not excuse dropping classification from
the published Change Request. Direct/Deliberative and local publication authority
continue to decide the verdict and permitted action.
