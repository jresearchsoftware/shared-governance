## Five semantic acceptance walks

These desk-based applications use the candidate and the stated scenario facts,
without the old project's residual rules. They establish coverage and decisions,
not live Skill installation, downstream adoption, independent acceptance or fresh
installed-model execution.

1. **Manual project, no Relay, one publishing account.** A tools project's owner
   authorizes Issue 42 / Step 1 export recovery, retaining an environment check.
   The short handoff links that Issue. The implementer preserves revision A and
   reports local checks plus unavailable environment evidence. A separate
   evaluator assesses A against the Issue; the owner copies the attributed report.
   Applying Exact result and independent assessment leaves native distinct-identity
   approval unproved. If the local policy requires it, acceptance waits; if no
   evaluator exists, independent acceptance stays pending. The remaining check
   keeps the Task open. **Semantic walk: PASS**; no product schema or Relay service
   is needed, and one-account publication is not promoted into identity proof.
2. **Project with a version-compatible Relay Skill.** The consumer separately
   adopts accepted shared guidance, selects an accepted Relay revision and qualifies
   its client/Skill mapping. The shared title/handoff/result invariants apply;
   the selected product contract remains the only execution/publication authority.
   A stale or incompatible adapter stops before launch rather than importing the
   report's old Request syntax. A Skill supplies neither credentials nor reviewer
   acceptance. **Semantic walk: PASS**; the three compatibility questions remain
   separate. No actual Skill, current Relay adapter or consumer is installed/tested.
3. **Renamed and continued multi-stage work.** A project explicitly binds Step to
   meaningful stages and permits conversation reuse. Current authority admits
   Step 2 qualification of Step 1 revision A. Refresh the Issue and accepted review
   frontier, retain A's link, and name the chat
   `Task 42 — Step 2 — Validate export recovery`. A supported persisted-title
   readback can establish the rename; unavailable rename/readback is disclosed
   with the Task link as navigation. Approval and inner fixes allocate no new unit;
   Step 2 alone cannot establish Task completion. **Semantic walk: PASS**; an
   attempt-based project's explicit different binding also remains valid.
4. **New product field, unchanged shared meaning.** A later accepted product adds
   diagnostic `trace_id` through its schema/parser/publisher/adapter change.
   Do not append it to an older incompatible record. Exact result, warnings,
   substantive review, identity limits and permission remain unchanged, so shared
   task-handoff needs no release solely for that field. Suppressing warnings,
   changing approval authority or adding mandatory owner work instead needs the
   applicable owner decision. **Semantic walk: PASS**; technical syntax stays with
   the product while material semantic changes remain governed.
5. **Classification requires deeper theory.** A proposed universal stage rule
   would force retries to reuse Step; a declared attempt binding numbers fresh
   admitted execution after a blocked attempt. Distinguish an inner correction,
   fresh execution and uncertain publication replay before prescribing a counter.
   Inner corrections/replay retain the identified work/result; fresh execution
   follows current local authorization/allocation. Inspect uncertain prior mutation
   before repeating it. Issue 14 already leaves stage/attempt policy with the
   project; an unresolved material local choice goes to its owner.
   **Semantic walk: PASS**; no new counter, duplicated analysis or ledger is added.
