Full G1 belongs to a future, separately admitted **contract-change/review
governance extraction**: detect out-of-scope accepted user/operator changes;
preserve behavior or stop during implementation; compare old/new behavior before
approval; and never normalize agent-preferred behavior retroactively into authority.
That extraction must include the requirement-origin distinction and may reuse
the common rule authored here. This Task neither implements nor activates the
review procedure. Its future implementation is not a completion prerequisite here.

and authoritative. [Relay Task 97](https://github.com/jresearchsoftware/codex-relay/issues/97)
may later transfer only demonstrably equivalent obligations and preserve its
product-specific bindings. Updating proportional-controls does not authorize
deleting G1 or classifying missing reusable obligations as a local overlay.
